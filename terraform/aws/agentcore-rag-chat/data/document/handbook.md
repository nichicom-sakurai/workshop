# Employee Handbook (excerpt)

This is a fictional handbook used as sample document data for the RAG chat.

## Working hours

- Standard working hours are 09:00–18:00 with a one-hour lunch break.
- Flexible start times between 07:00 and 10:00 are allowed with manager approval.
- Remote work is permitted up to three days per week.

## Paid time off

- Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Unused leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 10 days into the next year.
- Sick leave requires notifying your manager before 10:00 on the day of absence.

## Expense reimbursement

- Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase.
- Receipts are required for any expense over 10 USD.
- Travel booked through the company portal is billed directly and does not need reimbursement.
