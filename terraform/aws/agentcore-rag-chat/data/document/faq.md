# Internal FAQ (excerpt)

Fictional FAQ used as sample document data for the RAG chat.

**Q: How do I reset my single sign-on (SSO) password?**
A: Visit the identity portal, choose "Forgot password", and follow the email link. Passwords expire every 90 days.

**Q: How do I request access to a new internal tool?**
A: Open a ticket in the IT service desk with the tool name and business justification. Most requests are approved within one business day.

**Q: What is the guest Wi-Fi network?**
A: The network name is "Guest-WS" and the rotating password is posted in each meeting room.

**Q: Who do I contact for laptop hardware issues?**
A: Email it-support@example.com or open an IT service desk ticket. Loaner laptops are available at the front desk.
